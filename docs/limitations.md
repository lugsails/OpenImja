# Limitations and responsible use

OpenImja is an experimental public observation layer for Imja Tsho. It is **not** an operational flood-warning system and does not estimate breach probability, evacuation need, or danger level.

Satellite observations can be delayed, obscured by cloud, incomplete, misclassified, or wrong. A lake-area estimate is not a water-level, volume, dam-stability, weather, or downstream-impact measurement. Freshness labels only express the age of an observation; they have no hazard meaning.

For emergency information, warnings, and instructions, rely on Nepal's official authorities and local emergency management channels. Do not use this repository as the basis for safety-critical decisions.

The initial AOI, seed point, thresholds, and boundary selection all require expert review. Historical records should not be compared across sensors or method versions without considering their differing resolution, masks, seasonality, and uncertainty.
